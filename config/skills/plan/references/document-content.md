# Document and content definition

Use this adapter for reports, proposals, profiles, presentations, research deliverables, and repeatable content systems.

## Required analysis

- Define audience, intended decision or response, distribution context, format, length, language, tone, and deadline.
- Identify authoritative sources, evidence requirements, confidentiality, citation needs, and claims requiring validation.
- Define required sections, narrative order, visual or brand constraints, reusable assets, and review stakeholders.
- Separate factual accuracy, editorial quality, visual quality, and conversion or persuasion objectives.
- Define what is explicitly excluded and how approval will be measured.

## Definition additions

Add audience and outcome statement, source plan, content architecture, format constraints, approval rubric, evidence requirements, and production workstreams.
