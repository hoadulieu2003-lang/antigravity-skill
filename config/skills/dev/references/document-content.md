# Document and content delivery

- Build from approved audience, outcome, sources, structure, format, and review rubric.
- Partition research, structure, drafting, fact checking, editing, and visual production only when handoffs preserve source traceability and narrative coherence.
- One integrator or editor owns the final voice, hierarchy, terminology, and removal of duplication.
- Distinguish source-backed claims from assumptions or persuasive framing.
- Render and visually inspect deliverables when layout is part of acceptance.

Evidence may include source map, outline coverage, fact-check results, editorial review, visual QA, versioned final artifact, and rubric score.
